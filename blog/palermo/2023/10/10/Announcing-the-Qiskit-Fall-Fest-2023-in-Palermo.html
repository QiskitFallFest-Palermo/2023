Announcing the Qiskit Fall Fest 2023 in Palermo

← back to blog Published 10 Oct 2023 · Alberto Miserendino

We are thrilled to invite you to the Qiskit Fall Fest 2023 partner event in Palermo, Italy!

The Qiskit Fall Fest is a collection of quantum computing events on college campuses around the world. Every Fall Fest event is organized and run by the students on each campus, with support from the entire IBM Quantum team. Student leaders work alongside IBM throughout the month of September to design and plan their event, which are then run in October. The events vary from technical hackathons to beginner-friendly challenges, all with the goal of helping to grow the local community’s skill set.
https://medium.com/qiskit

📢 Stay tuned for registration details, speaker announcements, and more exciting updates on our website. Follow us on our socials for the latest news and discussions using our official hashtag #qiskitfallfest23palermo.

📅 Mark your calendars for 24 Nov 2023, 27 Nov 2023, and 29 Nov 2023, and join us in Palermo, Italy, for an unforgettable quantum computing experience. Save the dates and prepare for Qiskit Fall Fest 2023!

Don’t miss this opportunity to be part of the quantum revolution at Qiskit Fall Fest 2023. Whether you’re a quantum enthusiast, researcher, student, or industry professional, there’s something for everyone. We look forward to welcoming you to Palermo for a quantum celebration like no other!
